Youth voice on the global plastics treaty

As a member of the Youth Plastic Action Network (YPAN), working through the Children and Youth Major Group to UNEP, I take part in youth engagement on the treaty to end plastic pollution.

What YPAN is

The Youth Plastic Action Network (YPAN) organises young people around the global negotiations for a treaty to end plastic pollution. It works with the Children and Youth Major Group (CYMG) to the UN Environment Programme (UNEP), the official channel for young people's participation in UNEP processes.

Why it matters to my work

Plastic pollution is a systems problem: production, product design, waste collection, informal workers and finance all interact. That is the same kind of problem I work on in payments and data. Waste pickers and informal recyclers, for example, are among the people the formal financial system leaves out, and fair, traceable digital payments are part of any just transition for them.

What I bring to the table

  • Data and systems thinking from biometry and informatics: turning messy evidence into clear options.
  • Digital payments experience from implementing payment systems in Kenya: relevant to deposit-return schemes, extended producer responsibility fees and payments to waste workers.
  • A Global South, youth perspective grounded in Nairobi, where UNEP is headquartered.
These are my own views and do not represent YPAN, CYMG or UNEP.
