Disease Prediction Toolkit

An end-to-end machine-learning pipeline that predicts heart-disease risk from real clinical data, built in the DevTown “Disease Prediction: Save Lives with AI” bootcamp.

What I built

  • Data: the UCI Heart Disease dataset (real-world clinical records), cleaned and prepared with pandas and NumPy.
  • Feature engineering: encoding, scaling and selection of the clinical variables.
  • Models: logistic regression, decision tree and random forest, trained and compared in scikit-learn.
  • Evaluation: accuracy, precision, recall and confusion matrices, with visualisations and documentation.

Why it matters

Non-communicable diseases such as heart disease cause most premature deaths in low- and middle-income countries. Simple, explainable models can help flag risk early, where specialists are scarce. The same discipline (clean data, honest evaluation, explainable output) runs through my BioCredit work.
