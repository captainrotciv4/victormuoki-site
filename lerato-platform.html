Lerato Platform

A management platform I designed, built and deployed for a youth sports academy and its foundation: admissions, attendance, beneficiaries and finance in one system.

What it does

  • Learner admissions: structured learner records with admission numbers and guardian details.
  • Programme attendance: session-by-session attendance across programmes.
  • Beneficiaries and programmes: one source of truth for who is served and how.
  • Finance: finance movements and allocations, tracked against programmes.
  • Role-based access: staff sign-in, so personal data stays private.

How it is built

LayerChoice
AppNext.js 15 (React), TypeScript
DataPostgreSQL with Prisma; 44 data models
HostingNetlify, with automated database backups
Reliability and securityError monitoring, edge protection, staff-only access

The wider roll-out

Alongside the platform I planned the foundation's Year-1 IT set-up for about 78 staff: software licences, domain email, endpoint security, a backup strategy and supplier quotations, plus programme reporting.

The platform holds learners' personal data, so it is behind a staff sign-in. A guided demo is available on request.
